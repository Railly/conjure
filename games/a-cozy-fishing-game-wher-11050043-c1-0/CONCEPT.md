Title: Whale-Weight Aquarium
Genre: physics puzzle
Pitch: A wobbly physics puzzler where the friend on your hook completely changes how it swings, stretches, or floats. Plan each release to stack a whale, an octopus, and their pals safely into one tippy aquarium.
Core mechanic: Every friend you catch changes the physics of the next drop. A dangling whale turns your hook into a wrecking-ball pendulum, a clingy octopus stretches the line like a bungee, and a jellyfish floats upward against the fall - so you must release each friend at just the right swing to arc them gently into the right tier of a stackable glass aquarium, heavy friends low or the whole tank tips.
Controls: Left/Right or A/D: Move boat | Up/Down or W/S: Shorten or lengthen line | Space: Drop hook, then press again to release | Enter: Start
Win: Place all 6 friends in the right tiers in 12 drops or fewer - 'EVERYONE'S HOME! BEAUTIFULLY BALANCED.'
Lose: Tip the tank, land three friends too hard, or use all 12 drops - 'THE TANK TIPPED! TRY AGAIN.'
Roles: boat: Your movable crane boat at the top, carrying the hook, line, and winch; whale: The heaviest catch and bottom-tier resident - on the hook he swings wide and slow, and placed high he will tip the tank; octopus: A stretchy catch whose clinging tentacles lengthen your line mid-swing, and a middle-tier resident who can grab and steady a rocking tank once; lighthouse: The harbormaster on the shore - its beam flashes yellow, then red, to warn of the wind gust that hits on the next drop; jellyfish: A floaty catch who rises instead of falling when released, needing the top tier, and who drifts upward if you mistime her
Juice: Title screen: 'WHALE-WEIGHT AQUARIUM' with the whale dangling from the hook, swaying the whole title. Good landings make a soft splash, heart bubbles, a squash-and-stretch plop, and 'SOFT LANDING! +100'. Hard hits crackle 'TOO HARD!' with a screen shake and the tank rocking; a perfect gentle drop at the swing's peak trails sparkles and pops 'SPLASH-FREE! COMBO x3'.
Palette: Pale paper white, crayon red boat and octopus, soft blue whale, and sunny yellow lighthouse beam
