Title: Bounce Bot: Cloud Key Quest
Genre: platformer
Pitch: Bounce the Robot across drifting Clouds to grab the Key and unlock the Door — but watch out: Hedgehogs pop the Clouds right under your feet. Every landing feels like a tiny trampoline party, and every close call earns a poof of sparkles.
Core mechanic: You steer the Robot to jump between Clouds. The Clouds are solid platforms that sink, stretch, and snap back like soft trampolines. Grab the Key and the exit Door opens — in a whiffle-style mini platformer where timing and bouncing matter more than walking.
Controls: #robot-platformer-controls
Win: You win when the Robot carries the Key to the Door on the far cloud and steps through it.
Lose: You lose if the Robot falls off the bottom of the screen three times, or touches a Hedgehog twice — he pops into a cloud of screws, drops back to the last solid Cloud he stood on, and loses a heart. After the last heart, it's game over.
Roles: Robot: The player character — a cheerful crayon robot with antennae who runs, jumps, and squash-and-stretches between cloud platforms.; Cloud: The platforms themselves — drifting, bouncy cloud trampolines the Robot hops between; they sink under his weight and wobble back into shape.; Key: The goal item floating over a high cloud — grab it and the exit Door unlocks with a golden flash.; Door: The exit door standing on the final cloud at the far end of the level — walk through it with the Key to win.; Hedgehog: The hazard — spiky crayon hedgehogs patrolling certain clouds; touching one pops the Robot (and sometimes the cloud).
Juice: - Game feel: fast responsive jumps with coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump height, and a strongly eased landing squish that makes hopping between Clouds feel great.
- Reward feedback: every safe landing kicks out a puff of cloud particles; grabbing the Key pops gold sparkles and a wobble; reaching the Door swings it open with a slow creak and a star burst.
- Camera: camera-only scroll with slight lead — we look ahead in the direction the Robot is running so up-coming Hedgehogs and gaps are always readable.
- Music & SFX: a cheerful waltz-like mini tune with music-box melody over soft arps; landing poofs, a hollow trampoline boing-pitched by combo size, a shimmery Key pickup chime, and a Hedgehog "pop" sting.
- Visual: a flat crayon sky with big crayon stars and tiny gradient motes drifting behind; the Robot's crayon body, antennae balls, and circular eyes animate with squash-and-stretch and motion-trail afterimages.
Palette: Soft crayon sky: pale blue background, white clouds with light blue outlines, golden yellow key, warm brown door, and the Robot's white-and-light-blue body with its red, pink, and yellow chest buttons.
