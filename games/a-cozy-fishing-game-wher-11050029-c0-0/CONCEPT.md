Title: Friend Tow
Genre: physics puzzle
Pitch: Cast a swinging hook from your little boat, tow your new sea friends home in a wobbly chain, and fill your aquarium before sunset. The bigger your chain, the wilder it swings — and the whale is a LOT of chain.
Core mechanic: Every friend you catch stays hanging on the hook and becomes part of the pendulum. A jellyfish makes the chain light and floaty, an octopus adds extra swinging tentacles that can grab one more friend, and the whale is so heavy it drags the whole chain into a slow, wide arc — so your best catch can also wreck your next cast. You have to learn the new swing in real time and deliver the whole wobbling chain to your aquarium tank on the dock without bonking it and shaking friends loose.
Controls: Left / Right or A / D: Slide the boat. Space (hold and release): Lower and reel the hook. Enter: Start / restart.
Win: Deliver enough sea friends to fill all 8 spots in the boat's aquarium before the 90-second timer ends.
Lose: Time runs out before the aquarium is full, or you lose too many friends to hard bonks.
Roles: boat: Your playable boat on the surface. It slides left and right and carries the swinging hook and the aquarium drop-off point on its deck.; whale: The heavyweight star catch. Worth the most room in the aquarium, but catching it makes your pendulum slow, long, and very hard to control.; octopus: A living grappling upgrade. While it hangs on the chain, its free tentacles can snag one extra passing friend for a risky double catch.; jellyfish: A floaty wild card. It makes the chain buoyant and bouncy, so it rises faster but bounces off rocks and the lighthouse beam post.; lighthouse: Stands on the dock beside the aquarium. Its slowly rotating beam is your delivery guide — drop the chain into the tank while the beam points at it for a bonus "Perfect Drop" — and its base is a solid obstacle you must not slam your friends into.
Juice: Bubble trails stream off the hook, splash rings burst when friends break the surface, and each catch pops with a squash-and-stretch wiggle and a floating "Gotcha!" or "Two in one tow!". A gentle screen sway and a soft "bloop" shake happen when the whale lands, pages of seaweed particles drift up on a perfect delivery, and the aquarium fills with animated friends who follow the hook as it passes. Title screen: the boat bobs under the words FRIEND TOW, with the whale, octopus, and jellyfish already hanging from the hook in a silly, swaying chain.
Palette: Ocean blues and crayon reds from the drawings, with warm yellow lighthouse light.
