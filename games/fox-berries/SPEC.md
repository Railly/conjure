# Fox & Berries (Zorro y Bayas)

Goal: A little fox hops between floating rocks over a pond to collect all 12 berries before night falls (60 seconds). The sky darkens and the sun sinks as the timer runs out.
Controls: Left/Right (or A/D) to run, Space/Up/W to jump, R/Enter to restart after winning or losing.
Win: Collect all 12 berries before the timer ends.
Lose: Night falls (timer reaches zero) or the fox falls into the pond below the rocks.
On screen: One 960x540 screen — sunset sky with sun, moon and stars, pond at the bottom, a chain of rocks at different heights, berries above the rocks, a pushable pebble, HUD with berry count, a night-progress bar and messages in Spanish. Game over / victory overlay with restart hint.

Art objects: fox, berry, rock, tree
