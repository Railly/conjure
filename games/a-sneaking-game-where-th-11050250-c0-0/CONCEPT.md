Title: Squeak in the Dark (wear the rug!)
Genre: Stealth (top-down, one room)
Pitch: A tiny mouse, a huge wedge of cheese, and one very light sleeper — the only shadow in this lamplit room is the rug, and you can wear it. Drag it, hide under it, and dash out for cheese bite by bite before the cat opens its eyes.
Core mechanic: The rug is your only portable shadow. Out in the lamp's big swinging circle of light, moving makes the cat's Wake Meter fill fast — but hold Space and your mouse vanishes into a slow, creeping rug-lump that can cross bright floor safely. Pop out to grab a cheese bite, then sneak it home with your little feet sticking out from under the rug, moving 30% faster and filling the meter 30% faster each trip. Every 20 seconds the lamp swings wider and faster, so later trips are a gamble: slow and safe under the rug, or fast and exposed.
Controls: PRESS ARROW KEYS or WASD to tiptoe, HOLD SPACE to pull the rug over you, RELEASE SPACE to pop out, press ENTER to start
Win: You win by carrying all 3 cheese bites back to your mouse hole before the cat wakes.
Lose: You lose if the Wake Meter fills — the cat's eyes snap open and it pounces on the mouse.
Roles: Mouse: You — the player character, a big hand-drawn sprite that tiptoes, freezes, and disappears under the rug; Lamp: The danger zone — a swinging floor lamp whose moving cone of light is drawn over the whole room; moving inside it wakes the cat; Cat: The sleeping threat in the corner — its twitching ears and rising chest show the Wake Meter before it ever opens its eyes; Text (Zzz): The sleep gauge — big blue Zzz's float up from the cat; the faster and smaller they come, the closer it is to waking; Cheese: The prize — one giant wedge you carry home in three bites, each bite making the next run riskier; Rug: Your hiding place — the red rug you drag and wear as a mobile shadow, drawn large and center-stage
Juice: Title screen: the huge sleeping cat curled around the words SQUEAK IN THE DARK, one Zzz bobbing up and down, the rug-lump creeping across the bottom whenever you press an arrow. Juice: dust motes sparkle inside the lamp beam, soft paw-pad particles puff on carpet and loud yellow 'squeak!' stars burst on bare floor, the mouse squash-and-stretches with each tiptoe step and flattens completely when the rug drops on him, the cat's whiskers twitch and its tail thumps with a tiny screen shake as the Wake Meter climbs, and each cheese delivered pops a floating '+1 BITE!' in cheesy yellow. When the cat wakes, the lamp flares white, the screen shakes hard, and the cat uncurls in one big stretchy pounce.
Palette: Warm midnight-parlor colors pulled from the drawings themselves: cozy amber and butter-yellow lamplight on a deep blue-brown wooden floor, the red striped rug as the single bold splash of color, the mouse in soft pencil grays with a pink nose, the cat in warm orange tabby stripes, the cheese in bright holey yellow, and the sleepy Zzz's in pale crayon blue.
