# Penguin Ice — Empuja y Cruza

**Goal:** A waddling penguin must reach the igloo on the right side of a frozen lake.
The ice is cracked by deep water holes. Push ice blocks across the slippery
surface: when a block slides into a hole it sinks, plugs it flush, and becomes
safe ground. Fill the holes, collect fish for bonus points, and walk to the igloo.
Clear 3 levels to win.

**Controls:**
- Left / Right (arrows or A/D): waddle left or right, pushing one ice block at a time.
- R (restart input): restart the current level.
- Action (Space/Enter): start the next level, or play again after win / game over.

**Win:** Plug enough holes to cross and touch the igloo door on all 3 levels.
**Lose:** Fall into open water 3 times (3 lives). Falling costs a life and sends
the penguin back to the start of the level; filled holes stay filled.

**On screen:** Side view of one frozen lake per level: pale sky, falling snow,
distant ice hills, a blue-white ice floor with dark water holes, the penguin,
pushable translucent ice blocks, collectible fish, and the igloo at the far
right. HUD shows score, level, lives (hearts-as-text), holes filled and fish.
