Title: Sunset Beat Farm
Genre: rhythm or timing
Pitch: It's a cozy farming rhythm game where every carrot grows to the beat of the barn's drum before the sun goes down. Water on the beat, harvest on the beat, and don't let the chickens cluck you off tempo!
Core mechanic: You water only on the barn's drumbeat: a PERFECT hit grows a carrot two stages at once, a GOOD hit grows it one stage, and an off-beat pour just splashes the girl's boots. Ripe carrots take one more on-beat tap to launch them into the barn, and every harvest builds your combo multiplier. It starts at an easy garden tempo, but as the sun sinks the beat speeds up from a stroll to a scramble, and more chickens wander across the rows, clucking tempting off-beats a split-second early - trust the drum, not the chicken.
Controls:  Up/Down or W/S: Move the girl between the three soil rows. Space: Pour water or harvest a ripe carrot, right on the beat. Enter: Start or restart.
Win: Deliver 12 ripe carrots to the barn before the sun sets. The barn doors burst open with sparkles and the screen says "Harvest Complete! Great Timing, Farmer!"
Lose: The sun sets before 12 carrots reach the barn. The screen dims to dusk and shows "Sunset! You delivered X of 12 carrots. Press Enter to Try Again."
Roles: girl: The player character. She hops between the three soil rows and does all the watering and harvesting with a big musical splash.; carrot: The crop and the whole point of the game: carrots grow through three visible stages in the soil rows and fly into the barn when harvested.; wateringcan: The girl's tool, front and center: it pours an arc of water that only lands if you hit Space on the beat.; chicken: Wandering tricksters that stroll across the rows and cluck just before the real beat, trying to throw off your timing.; barn: The stage and the goal: a drum thumps from the barn door, the beat ring pulses around it, and harvested carrots land inside it.
Juice: Water droplets burst in time with the beat on every good pour, carrots squash down then spring up a size with a leafy pop, and the whole screen gives a soft pulse on PERFECT hits. Floating text pops up: "PERFECT!", "GOOD", "MISS", "Combo x8!", and a harvested carrot arcs into the barn trailing sparkles while the door wiggles. Chickens ruffle feathers and flash "BAWK!" when they nearly fool you. Title screen: the girl bobs under the big hand-drawn title "SUNSET BEAT FARM" while a carrot bounces on the beat and "Press Enter to Play" pulses below.
Palette: Warm sunset oranges and pinks over soft soil browns, leafy greens, and the bright crayon colors of the original drawings.
