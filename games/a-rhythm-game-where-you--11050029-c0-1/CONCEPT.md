Title: Froggy Finale
Genre: rhythm or timing
Pitch: Hit the falling notes in time with the bouncing frog and he dances a full concert routine. Keep the combo alive through the whole song and he pulls off the big finale leap!
Core mechanic: You are not playing to an invisible metronome — you are playing to the frog. He bounces on the drum, and every note is only PERFECT if you hit it on the exact frame he lands. Chain perfects and he does a bigger and bigger dance combo; break the chain and he slips, reverses his bounce for 4 beats, and the next notes come in mirrored lanes.
Controls: A/K for the guitar lane, S/L for the drum lane, Space to make the frog stomp on a glowing beat. Hit pairs with two lane keys at once. Enter starts.
Win: Survive to the end of the 90-second song: 'BRAVO! What A Dance!'
Lose: Miss 8 notes total and the show stops: 'The Crowd Went Quiet...'
Roles: guitar: The left instrument lane — its neck is the track A/K notes slide down, and it strums and shakes on every hit.; note: The standard black note — a single quick tap on the guitar or drum lane.; drum: The right instrument lane and the frog's trampoline — he bounces on it to set the true beat, and S/L notes land on its head.; pair: The beamed double note — must be hit with one guitar key and one drum key at the same time.; tone: The second single note, drawn bigger and slower — a hold note: press and keep holding until its tail passes.; frog: The star dancer and living metronome in the center — his landing frame defines PERFECT timing, and he performs the dance your combo builds.
Juice: Title screen: 'FROGGY FINALE — Press Enter, Feel The Beat!' with the frog bouncing on the big drum under the title. Perfect hits burst tiny stars off the note, squash the frog flat then stretch him tall, and pop floating text: 'PERFECT!', 'GREAT!', 'MISS...', 'COMBO x12!'. The guitar wobbles and sheds sparkles when its lane is hit, the drum booms with a ring shockwave and tiny screen shake on every frog landing. A 10-perfect streak trails rainbow notes behind the frog's feet.
Palette: Warm stage browns and reds from the guitar and drum, black ink notes, and the bright green frog popping in the center spotlight.
